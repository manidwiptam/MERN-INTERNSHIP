Ryomen Sukuna

Ryomen Sukuna, widely known as the King of Curses, is a legendary and immensely powerful antagonist from the anime and manga series Jujutsu Kaisen. He was originally a human sorcerer over a thousand years ago during the Heian Era—the golden age of jujutsu—before transforming into a cursed object split across his twenty indestructible fingers.
